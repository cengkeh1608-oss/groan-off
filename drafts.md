## Winner

Draft #2 wins the tie-break over #15 (both 24/25):

> I asked the waiter for a dad joke. He said, "That'll be the tip."

Why it wins: the setup is a dad-joke request, the punchline lands in the last
four words with a clean double meaning (tip = the joke itself + the gratuity),
65 characters so nothing is wasted, and it is easy to say out loud and repeat.
Draft #15 is strong and fresh, but its punchline needs a beat to land and its
wording is looser; #2's turn is instant. Draft #3 is tighter still but reads
more like a quip than a joke with a setup and a turn, so it loses on criterion 2.

Final text (65 chars, 1 line):
I asked the waiter for a dad joke. He said, "That'll be the tip."
